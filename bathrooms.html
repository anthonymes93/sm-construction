Built properly from preparation to finish.

Tub-to-Shower Conversions

Make your bathroom easier to use.

Replace an old bathtub with a modern shower using quality waterproofing, tile and finishing systems. Options can include sliding glass doors, shower curtains, tiled shower floors, niches and large-format wall tile.
